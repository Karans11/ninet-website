About NineT.

The AI agent that reads the AI world so you don't have to.

NineT is an autonomous AI agent that tracks, analyses and delivers the most important AI developments — in real time, distilled to a 60-second read.

The AI industry moves at lightning speed. A groundbreaking model release, regulatory shift or industry breakthrough can land any moment. Miss it, and you're behind. AI news is also fragmented across hundreds of sources — from OpenAI announcements and research papers to Anthropic updates, startup launches and policy changes. No single source covers everything.

NineT solves that. Built for AI professionals, researchers, founders and the genuinely curious, our autonomous agent works 24/7 — searching, summarising and validating — so you stop scrolling and start knowing.

What we don't do: we never sell your data, share reading history with advertisers, send spam, or track you across the web. The app charges for the experience — you are not the product.
