About NineT

The AI agent that reads the AI world so you don't have to.

NineT is an autonomous AI agent that tracks, analyses and delivers the most important artificial intelligence developments — in real time, distilled to a 60-second read.

Built for AI professionals, researchers, founders and the genuinely curious, NineT eliminates the time-consuming task of manually searching multiple sources for AI developments.

The AI industry moves at lightning speed. A groundbreaking model release, regulatory shift or industry breakthrough can land any moment. Miss it, and you're behind. AI news is also fragmented across hundreds of sources — from OpenAI announcements and research papers to Anthropic updates, startup launches and policy changes. No single source covers everything.

The problem with AI news today

Professionals spend hours daily searching Google News, Reddit, X, LinkedIn and countless AI blogs just to stay current. Even when you find the news, you're flooded with redundant articles, clickbait headlines and lengthy reports. What matters? What's noise? It's exhausting to filter.

Generic AI newsletters send everything or nothing. They don't understand your specific interests — whether you focus on LLMs, computer vision, AI safety, AI tools or AI business applications.

How NineT solves it

NineT is not just another news app — it's an intelligent AI agent that works autonomously to keep you informed about artificial intelligence developments without manual effort. Our agent searches, summarises and validates news 24/7. Every article passes a hallucination check before publishing. Each summary lands in ~80 words. Every story can be read in 60 seconds.

What we don't do

We never sell your personal data to third parties. We don't share your reading history with advertisers. We don't send spam or unsolicited marketing emails. We don't track you across other websites. The app charges for the experience — you are not the product.
