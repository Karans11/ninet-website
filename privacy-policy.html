Educational Project & Free Service Notice

NineT is an educational project provided completely free of charge. This application is developed as a learning project to demonstrate mobile app development, AI integration and software engineering practices. While we implement production-grade security measures, users acknowledge the educational nature of this project. Features may be experimental and support is provided on a best-effort basis.

1. General

1.1 Ninet.io ("NineT", "We", "Our", "Us") is committed to the protection of user ("You", "Your", "User") provided information which personally identifies You ("Personal Information") to NineT. You agree that Your use of NineT's mobile application ("App") implies Your consent to collecting, receiving, possessing, storing, dealing or handling of Your Personal Information in accordance with the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1.2 This Privacy Policy applies to all Users who access the App and are therefore required to read and understand the Privacy Policy before submitting any Personal Information.

1.3 We take the privacy of our Users seriously. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of Our Users while providing a personalized and valuable service.

1.4 Access to the contents available through the App is conditional upon Your approval of this Privacy Policy which is in addition to the Terms of Service.

2. Information Collected

2.1 Traffic Data Collected

In order to provide the App, We automatically track and collect the following categories of information when You use the App:

  • IP addresses
  • Domain servers
  • Device information (device model, operating system, app version)
  • Network information (WiFi/cellular status)
  • Other information with respect to Your device, interaction of Your device with the App and applications (collectively "Traffic Data")

2.2 Personal Information Collected

In order to provide the App, We may require You to provide Us with Personal Information. Personal Information includes:

  • Contact data (such as Your email address and display name)
  • Device data (device identifiers for authentication)
  • Usage data (articles read, reading time, bookmarks, search queries, reading streaks)
  • Preference data (such as Your article preferences, notification settings and display preferences)

If You communicate with Us by email or in-app feedback, any information provided in such communication may be collected and stored by NineT.

Our App may transmit Your Personal Information to our internal servers. This Personal Information is deleted from our servers 30 days after You delete the App or cancel/terminate your user account. Backup copies are retained for 90 days for disaster recovery purposes.

2.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Precise location data (GPS coordinates)
  • Contacts from Your device
  • Photos, videos or media files
  • Microphone or camera access
  • Financial or payment information
  • Social media account information

2.4 Third-Party Links

Our App may contain links to third party websites or applications. The inclusion or exclusion of the link does not imply any endorsement by NineT. You agree and understand that privacy policies of these websites are not under Our control. NineT encourages You to read the privacy policies of each such website.

3. AI-Powered Features

3.1 Artificial Intelligence Usage

The App uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology to enhance Your experience:

  • Content curation and personalization based on Your reading patterns
  • Article categorization and topic matching
  • Smart content filtering

3.2 AI Data Processing

The AI systems process Your usage data (articles read, time spent, categories engaged with, search queries) to provide personalized recommendations. This processing is automated and helps deliver content matched to Your interests.

3.3 AI Limitations

AI recommendations are automated and may not always be accurate. We actively work to minimize bias in content curation. You should verify important information from original sources.

4. Usage of Personal Information

4.1 Purpose of Collection

The information collected from You is used to ensure services with respect to the App are presented in the most effective manner, to carry out Our obligations to You and to communicate with You via push notifications, emails and transactional or informational messages.

4.2 Permitted Use

In general, We use Our best efforts to use information in aggregate form. However, We may use Your Personal Information for the following purposes:

  • To provide personalized article recommendations
  • To enable core app functionality (authentication, bookmarks, reading progress)
  • To aid strategic development, data collection and business analytics
  • To enable Us to provide the App through appropriate technological services
  • To audit usage of the App and identify technical issues
  • To enhance user experience in relation to the App
  • For educational research purposes using only anonymized, aggregated data

4.3 Educational Research

As this is an educational project, anonymized and aggregated data may be used in portfolio presentations, case studies or technical documentation. Personal identifiable information is never used in educational contexts.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

5.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We do not disclose Your Personal Information to any third parties other than to NineT's service providers or other trusted business partners pursuant to a lawful contract. Our service providers include:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication services
  • Cloudflare — API hosting and security
  • Expo / EAS — App distribution and updates
  • Apple / Google — Push notification services

5.2 No Sale of Data

No User information is rented or sold to any third party. We do not sell, rent or trade Your Personal Information for commercial purposes.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose Your Personal Information if required by law, court order, government regulation, or to protect Our rights, safety and property.

5.4 Business Transfer

In the event of a merger, reorganization, acquisition, joint venture, assignment, spin-off, transfer or sale of all or any portion of Our educational project, You will be notified 30 days in advance via email and will have the option to export Your data, delete Your account before transfer, or continue under new ownership with updated terms.

6. Data Security

6.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3)
  • Encryption of data at rest (AES-256)
  • Password hashing (bcrypt with salt)
  • Secure authentication (JWT tokens)
  • Regular security monitoring and updates
  • Access controls and logging

6.2 Data Breach

In the event of a data breach affecting Your Personal Information, We will notify You within 72 hours via email and provide information about the breach, affected data and steps We are taking.

6.3 Your Responsibility

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of Your account credentials and for all activities under Your account.

7.2 Account Deletion

User can delete the account directly from the app and also send the email to us. Upon deletion request:

  • Personal data deleted from primary database within 30 days

  • Anonymized analytics may be retained
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed

8. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of Your Personal Information
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Delete Your account and all associated data
  • Export — Download Your data in JSON / CSV format
  • Opt-Out — Disable AI recommendations, notifications or analytics
  • Withdraw Consent — Revoke permissions at any time

How to exercise rights

Email contact@ninet.io. Response time within 30 days. We may verify Your identity before processing requests.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The mobile App does NOT use traditional web cookies. We use:

  • Local Storage — to save preferences and offline articles (essential)
  • Session Tokens — to keep You logged in securely (essential)
  • Device Identifiers — for authentication (essential)
  • Analytics IDs — to track app usage patterns (optional, can be disabled)

You can clear local storage through App Settings → Storage → Clear Cache.
